Why Age 7? The Optimal Window

Our Approach

We start at age 5 with Scratch Jr (visual tablet programming), progress to WeDo 2.0 / SPIKE Essential (icon-based LEGO robotics) at 7, then build up through word blocks, Python, and Arduino by age 13+.
